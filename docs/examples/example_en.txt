Once upon a time there were three little pigs. The first pig built a house of straw. The second pig built a house of sticks. The third pig built a house of bricks. A hungry wolf came and huffed and puffed and blew the house of straw down. Then the wolf huffed and puffed and blew the house of sticks down. But the wolf could not blow the house of bricks down. So the wolf ran away, and the three little pigs were safe.
